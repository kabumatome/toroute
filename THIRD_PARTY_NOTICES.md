# Third-party notices

The container installs Tor, Privoxy, obfs4proxy, Tini, CA certificates, and
runtime dependencies from Debian. Those components retain their own licenses
and copyright notices under `/usr/share/doc/*/copyright` in the image.

The source archive's Apache-2.0 license applies only to ToRoute's original
source. Review the generated SBOM and Debian copyright files for each release.
